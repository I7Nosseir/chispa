You're third for patient volume, but you have the lowest rating and the highest share of one-star reviews of the six. That's what people compare when they choose.

4 fixes, in order

1
Your address shows GPS coordinates, not a street address. The listing reads "29.960535, 31.290359 Zahraa Al Maadi…". A patient can't tell a driver where to go. Fix: enter the street, building and a landmark. About 10 minutes.
2
Nothing is linked: no WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook or website. Someone who finds you has nowhere to see your treatments or book. Fix: add a WhatsApp booking link and your social pages now; a simple one-page site with treatments and booking later.
3
19 one-star reviews pull your rating down to 4.6. Fix: a calm, professional reply under each one (templates ready), and a QR review card at reception so satisfied patients leave a review. Roughly 70 new five-star reviews would lift you to about 4.7.
4
Show what you treat. Add your services on Google (back pain, sports injuries, post-surgery rehab and so on) and photos of the clinic and team. No patient photos without written consent.
