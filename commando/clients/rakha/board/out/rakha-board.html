OVERVIEW

MR RAKHA is the IGCSE and O-level Business and Economics class of Mr Mohamed Rakha, a teacher with over ten years in the classroom. He explains the syllabus through the real world — the prices, brands and headlines students already know — so business stops being memorised and starts being understood. Calm, witty and precise, he teaches students to think like a CEO and write like an examiner.

The signet: for centuries a ring pressed into wax sealed what was genuine and approved. Mr Rakha's mark is that seal on every lesson.
