# How to approach without sounding like a cold caller

**The idea in one line:** by the time you call, they should already know your name, because you've helped them once for free.

A cold caller asks for time. You arrive with something useful about *their* business and ask for nothing until they reply.

## The warm ladder (do the steps in order)
1. **Look before you touch (5 minutes).** Open their Google Maps and Instagram. Find the one real gap; the Command Center shows it for each business. Note the owner's or manager's name if the page shows it.
2. **Show up as a person, not a seller (days 1–2).**
   - Follow their Instagram. Like 2–3 recent posts.
   - Leave one real comment on a good post: a question about the food or the service, not "nice!".
   - If they're near you (Zayed, October, Pyramids Gardens), **go as a customer.** Buy something, chat with the staff, ask who runs the page.
3. **Give first (day 3).** Send the short note from the Copy Kit by WhatsApp or DM:
   - Name the one gap.
   - Offer the fix for free.
   - Ask permission to send it.
   - No price, no "services", no portfolio dump.
   - For Gulf product brands, the gift is an AI image of *their own* product.
4. **Deliver the gift fast (same day they reply).**
   - Send a one-page audit or the fix itself: a screenshot with the problem circled, plus 3 lines on how to fix it.
   - Make it with the AI audit prompt in the Copy Kit.
5. **Now call. It's a warm call.** "Hi, I'm Ismail, I sent you the note about [gap]. Did it make sense?" Then listen.
6. **Ask for 10 minutes in person, not for money.** Show their numbers on their phone. Agree on one small first step: a free trial week, one project, or one shoot day.
7. **Close on scope, not hype.** Agree the deliverables, price, dates and payment per phase. Get the deposit first. Get admin or partner access, never passwords.

## Rules that keep you warm
- **One niche per week.** By Friday you can say "I work with 5 dental clinics in Zayed" truthfully. That's your positioning.
- **Specific beats clever.** "The website button on your Google listing opens a Google Form" beats any slogan.
- **Never trash** their current marketer, a competitor or their reviews. Praise what works first.
- **Three touches, then stop:** note, follow-up, last note. Revisit next season (Ramadan, summer, back-to-school).
- **Use only public business numbers.** Respect "no".
- **Proof you may use (never inflate):**
  - Grapes Holiday: −62.5% ad spend, 2.5× leads, 3,000+ qualified leads.
  - Rihla Travel: 350K+ EGP in the first 2 weeks.
  - Later: La Margrita's and Rakha's results.

## Your daily rhythm (about 90 minutes)
| Time | Do |
|---|---|
| 10 min | Pick 5 businesses (one niche) in the Command Center. Read their gap. |
| 20 min | Warm up: follow, like and comment on 5 accounts. Visit one in person if it's on your way. |
| 30 min | Send 5 notes (the ones warmed up 2 days earlier). Run the AI audit prompt for anyone who replied. |
| 20 min | Calls: only people who replied. Book meetings. |
| 10 min | Log everything in the sheet (Status, Notes, Follow-up date). |

## What to say when they ask "who are you?"
"I'm Ismail. I run marketing for a few brands and businesses, including Grapes Holiday and Rihla Travel. I also build my own: Ignite Uni and Sparq Study. I'm local, I do the work myself, and you deal with me directly, not an account manager."
