# Calling playbook: The Commando agency (11 Oct 2026)

Goes with `commando/leads/leads-2026-10-11.xlsx` (100 businesses, sheet "Leads") and its CSV copy.
Write it in English, then say it in your own Egyptian Arabic. Don't read it word for word.

## Before you dial
- Open the business's Instagram and Google Maps on your phone first (links are in the sheet). Have the one gap from the "Gaps" column ready to say.
- Call Tier A first, in the morning (11 AM–1 PM). Restaurants and cafés: call 4–6 PM, never at lunch or dinner rush. Clinics: call outside clinic hours, or ask the receptionist when the doctor is free.
- One goal per call: **get the owner's name and WhatsApp, and a 10-minute meeting or permission to send a free audit.** You're not selling on the first call.
- Log every call in the sheet: Status, Call notes, Follow-up date.

## The 60-second call
1. "Hi, I'm Ismail from The Commando. I work with [clinics / restaurants / gyms] in [area]. Quick question: who handles your marketing or social media?"
2. If it's the owner, or someone who can decide: "I looked at [Business] before calling. Your reviews are strong ([X] on Google), but [one gap from the sheet]."
3. "That usually means people who already like you online end up calling someone else."
4. "I've done this for real businesses: for Grapes Holiday we cut ad spend 62% and got 2.5× the leads; Rihla Travel made 350K+ EGP in its first two weeks."
5. "Can I send you a free 2-minute audit on WhatsApp, showing exactly what I'd fix? No cost, no commitment."
6. "What's the best WhatsApp to send it to, and what's your name?"

## Why they'd work with you and not another marketer
Use the one that fits. The sheet has a tailored "Why you" line per business.
- **You do websites and marketing together.** Most freelancers do one. Agencies charge for both separately and hand them to juniors.
- **The owner deals with you directly,** not an account manager. You answer WhatsApp yourself.
- **Proof from real money:** Grapes Holiday (−62.5% ad spend, 2.5× leads), Rihla Travel (350K+ EGP in 2 weeks). Only say these numbers. Never round them up.
- **You show up with their problem already found** (the audit). Agencies show up with a price list.
- **AI production:** carousels, motion graphics and video editing made in-house, so more content for the same budget.

## If the person who answers won't pass you to the owner
This is normal. Receptionists are told to block sales calls. Don't push. Turn them into your helper.
1. **Ask for a name, not a transfer:** "No problem. What's the owner's or manager's name, so I address it to the right person?"
2. **Ask for the channel they use:** "Is there a WhatsApp number or email where management reads messages?" Most Egyptian businesses run on the business WhatsApp. That's your way in.
3. **Leave something that gets passed on:** "I found [specific gap, e.g. the phone number on your Instagram is wrong]. Could you tell the owner? I can send the details on WhatsApp." A real problem gets forwarded; a sales pitch doesn't.
4. **Ask when:** "When is the owner usually there? I'll call back then." Write it in Follow-up date.
5. **Go around:** DM the Instagram/Facebook page with the audit (owners often read DMs themselves), or walk in if it's near you (Zayed, October, Pyramids Gardens).
6. **Stop after 3 tries** (call, call back, WhatsApp/DM). Mark it "Not interested" and move on. There are 99 more.
- Never: lie about who you are, say "the owner asked me to call", or argue with the receptionist.

## If they say "we already have someone for social media"
Don't attack the person they have. Make it easy to say yes to something small.
1. **Agree:** "Great, that's good to hear."
2. **Find what they don't cover:** "Do they also handle the website, the Google Maps profile and replying to reviews? Most social media people don't."
   - Most of your leads have gaps there (no website, broken links, unanswered 1★ reviews). That's a separate job you can sell without replacing anyone.
3. **Offer a second opinion, not a replacement:** "Can I send a free audit anyway? If your person is doing a great job, it'll confirm it. If not, you'll know what to ask them for."
4. **Plant a timer:** "When does your contract with them come up for review?" Put that month in Follow-up date.
5. **Offer a project, not a retainer:** a website, a one-off video shoot day, an ads campaign for one season (Ramadan, back-to-school, summer). Projects get in the door. Retainers come later.
- If their content is actually good (the sheet says so), be honest: offer only the website or Google side.

## Other objections (short answers)
- **"How much?"** "It depends on what you need. Websites start around 12K EGP, social media from 6K a month. Let me send the audit first so you know if it's even worth it." (These are the proposed ranges in your profile. Confirm them before quoting.)
- **"Send me something."** "Sure. What's your WhatsApp? I'll send it today and call you Thursday to hear what you think."
- **"Business is fine, we don't need marketing."** "Clearly. Your reviews prove it. This is about the people who search online and never reach you. Can I just show you what they see?"
- **"We tried an agency, it didn't work."** "What happened?" Listen. Then: "That's why I start with a free audit and pay-by-phase. You only pay for what you see."
- **"No time."** "Totally fine. Morning or afternoon, which is better for 2 minutes this week?"

## What to ask for on the call (and before any work starts)
On the call:
- The decision-maker's name and WhatsApp
- Permission to send the free audit
- A 10-minute meeting (in person if close, or a video call)

In the meeting, before any work:
- **Their goal in one sentence** (more bookings? more delivery orders? more members?) and how they measure it today.
- **Baseline numbers:** orders, bookings or members per week, and where customers come from now.
- **Budget range:** "What do you spend on marketing today, roughly?" (including ad spend).
- **Who approves content** and how fast, plus one contact person.
- **What they've tried before** and what went wrong.
- **Brand assets:** logo files, colours, photos, menu or price list.
- **A signed scope:** deliverables, price, dates, revision rounds (2), and payment by phase (deposit / mid / final). **Get the deposit before you start.**
- **Access the safe way:** they add you as an admin or partner (Meta Business Suite, Google Business Profile, TikTok Business, Buffer). Their accounts stay theirs.
- **Permission to use the results** as a case study.

## What NOT to ask for, or do
- **Never ask for passwords** or their login codes. Use admin/partner access only.
- **Don't ask for payment cards** or to pay ads from your own card. They pay the ad budget directly.
- **Don't promise numbers** ("I'll get you 10K followers", "double your sales"). Promise the work and the reporting.
- **Don't start work before the deposit** (free trials like La Margrita are the exception, and need a written end date).
- **Don't trash their current marketer** or competitors.
- **Don't send a price list before you understand the goal.**
- **Don't ask for customer data** (phone lists, patient records). For clinics, never post patient photos without written consent.
- **Don't call the same place more than 3 times** without a reply.
- **Don't cold-call personal mobile numbers you found outside the business listing.** Only use the business number in the sheet.

## Follow-up WhatsApp (send the same day)
"Hi [Name], Ismail from The Commando. Thanks for your time today. As promised, here's the quick audit for [Business]: [gap 1], [gap 2]. Happy to walk you through the fix in 10 minutes. Does [day] at [time] work?"

## Cadence
- Day 0: call, then WhatsApp the audit.
- Day 2: one follow-up message.
- Day 7: one last call.
- After that, mark it and move on. Revisit in 3 months (new season).

Remember: only use the business numbers listed publicly, and respect it when someone says "don't call again".
