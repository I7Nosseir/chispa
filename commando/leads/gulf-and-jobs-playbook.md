## Gulf: message first, then call
Gulf owners and managers answer WhatsApp and Instagram DMs more than cold calls from a foreign number.
- **Channel order:** Instagram DM to the brand (if they have one), then WhatsApp to the business number, then email from the website. Call only once they've replied.
- **Timing:** UAE works Mon–Fri. Saudi works Sun–Thu. Send 10 AM–1 PM Gulf time (9 AM–12 PM Cairo). Avoid Friday midday prayers.
- **Language:** English for Dubai (many shop managers aren't Arab). Arabic for Saudi and Qatar: write in plain, polite Arabic, not Egyptian slang.
- **Currency:** quote in USD (or AED/SAR if they ask). Never in EGP.

### First message (WhatsApp / DM): short, with a sample
"Hi [Brand] team, I'm Ismail, a performance marketer and AI content producer. I made this image of your [product] as a quick example of what we can do without a studio shoot (attached). I make product visuals and UGC-style video ads for Gulf brands and run the ads too. Want me to send 3 more for free so you can test them in your ads?"

Why it works: they see their own product before you ask for anything. The free part is small and capped at 3 images, not a free month.

### Follow-up (day 3)
"Hi, just checking you saw the [product] image. Happy to send the 3 free versions this week. Which product should I use?"

### Last follow-up (day 8)
"Last note from me. If product visuals or ad creatives become a priority, I'm here. Portfolio: [link]." Then stop.

### When they reply "how much?"
Give the pilot first, then the monthly price: "A starter pack is 10 product images + 3 short video ads for $[X]. Most brands then move to a monthly pack for fresh ad creatives every week." Use the Fair pricing sheet, and confirm those prices before quoting.

### "We already have an agency / photographer"
"Makes sense. Most brands I work with keep their photographer for hero shoots and use me for the weekly ad creatives the photographer can't produce fast enough. Want to test 3 against your current ads?"

## Remote jobs: how to apply
- **Apply with proof in the first two lines.** Recruiters skim. Open with Grapes Holiday: −62.5% spend, 2.5× leads, 3,000+ qualified leads.
- **Message the hiring manager too.** Find them on LinkedIn (Head of Marketing / Growth at the company). Send: "Hi [Name], I just applied for the [role]. Quick context: at Grapes Holiday I cut ad spend 62.5% while lead volume grew 2.5×. Happy to show how in 10 minutes."
- **Attach the portfolio link, not a big PDF.** The CV is one page; the portfolio does the rest.
- **For AI content roles:** send a 3-image or 1-video test made for their brand before the interview. It beats any CV.
- **Part-time first:** you're studying at GUC and running clients. A part-time remote role (like Whitecollars) pays in a stronger currency and keeps time for clients.
- **Red flags:** any recruiter asking for a fee, ID scans before an interview, or "training costs". Walk away. (Listing #6 needs checking for this reason.)

## What to ask for, and what not to ask for
**Clients (before work):** a deposit (50% for packs under $500, otherwise phased), the products to shoot (photos or samples), brand assets, one approver, admin/partner access to their ad account, and permission to show the work.
**Jobs:** salary in USD/AED/SAR, how you'll be paid (bank transfer or Payoneer), hours/time zone, the contract type, and the ad budget you'll manage.
**Don't:** ask for or share passwords, pay ad budgets from your card, promise ROAS numbers, accept "commission only", do more than one free test per brand, or quote in EGP to Gulf clients.
