# How to approach (the simple version)

One page. Read it before your first call. The full scripts are in `leads/calling-playbook.md` (Egypt) and `leads/gulf-and-jobs-playbook.md` (Gulf and jobs). Every message is ready to copy in `reports/copy-paste-kit.html`.

## The one idea
**A cold caller asks for something. You bring something.**
Every call or message opens with one true thing you noticed about *their* business, and an offer to send a free fix. You are a neighbour who spotted a problem, not a salesperson with a price list.

## Before every contact (2 minutes)
1. Open their Google Maps listing and Instagram on your phone.
2. Pick **one** gap from the sheet and check it's still true (for example "the website button opens a Google Form").
3. Have one result ready to mention: Grapes Holiday cut ad spend 62.5% and got 2.5× the leads.

## Egypt: the call in 4 steps
1. **Who you are, in one line:** "Hi, I'm Ismail from The Commando. I work with [clinics] in [area]."
2. **The thing you noticed:** "I was looking at your Google page. Your reviews are excellent, but [the one gap]."
3. **Ask permission instead of pitching:** "Can I send you a 2-minute audit on WhatsApp showing how to fix it? It's free."
4. **Get the name and the WhatsApp.** That's the whole goal of the call. Stop there.

Then send the WhatsApp **the same day**. Most deals come from this follow-up, not from the call.

## Gulf: message first, never call first
1. Make **one** AI image of their real product (using their real photo, never an invented label).
2. Send it with 3 lines: who you are, what you noticed, and "want 3 more free?".
3. Follow up on day 3, and send a last note on day 8. Then stop.
4. Call only after they reply.

## What makes you sound warm instead of cold
| Sounds cold | Sounds warm |
|---|---|
| "We offer social media management packages…" | "I noticed your website button opens a Google Form." |
| "Can I speak to the owner?" | "What's the owner's name, so I address it properly?" |
| Price in the first minute | Free audit first, price after you know their goal |
| Reading a script | Saying it in your own Egyptian Arabic |
| Calling at lunch rush | Clinics outside clinic hours, restaurants 4–6 PM |
| Arguing with "we already have someone" | "Great. Do they also handle the website and Google reviews?" |
| Calling 6 times | 3 tries (call, call back, WhatsApp), then move on |

## When they push back (one line each)
- **"We already have someone."** "Good to hear. I'll send the audit as a second opinion. If they're doing great, it'll confirm it."
- **"How much?"** "It depends on the goal. Let me send the audit first so you know if it's even worth it."
- **"Send me something."** "Sure. What's your WhatsApp? I'll send it today and call Thursday."
- **Receptionist blocks you:** ask for the owner's name, the best WhatsApp, and when they're usually in. Then leave the gap with them: a real problem gets passed on.

## Three ways in that aren't cold at all
1. **Walk in.** For Tier A places near you (Zayed, October, Hadayek), go in, buy something or ask a question, and mention the gap in person.
2. **Referrals.** Every client you sign: ask in week 2, "Who else do you know who has the same problem?"
3. **Content as proof.** Post your before/after audits and the AI samples (labelled "spec concept") on your own Instagram and LinkedIn. People who saw your work are already warm when you call.

## Never
- Ask for passwords. Use admin or partner access only.
- Promise numbers ("double your sales").
- Start work before the deposit.
- Trash their current marketer.
- Send an AI sample with an invented label, or present AI UGC as a real customer review.
