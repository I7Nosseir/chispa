# Pricing: first deals (17 Oct 2026)

For a solo freelancer selling to owner-run, single-location businesses (batch 1 "Go now" and "Pilot").

These are starting prices, set below agency rates but not cheap. The founder confirms them before quoting. Raise them 20–30% after 3 paying clients with before/after numbers.

The deal sizes in the lead sheets (website 20–35K EGP + social 10–20K/month) are for later. They're too high for a first deal with a new client.

## Egypt menu (EGP)
| Job | What's included | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Google listing fix** (the door-opener) | Correct links (website, Instagram, WhatsApp), hours, categories, 10 photos, replies to the last 20 reviews, a review QR card and WhatsApp review message | **1,500–2,500, one-time** |
| **Review care** | Reply to every new review within 48 hours, plus a monthly 1-page report (rating, review count, complaints) | **1,500–2,500 / month** |
| **One-page website** | Services and prices, WhatsApp booking button, map, linked from Google; 2 revision rounds | **6,000–10,000, one-time** (the client pays the domain and hosting, about 1,000–1,500/year) |
| **Full website (4–6 pages)** | Treatment, service or menu pages, booking, basic SEO | 12,000–20,000 |
| **Content starter** | 8 posts/reels a month, captions, posting | **5,000–8,000 / month** |
| **Content plus** | 12 reels plus stories, one shoot day a month | 8,000–12,000 / month |
| **Ads management** | Meta ads setup, weekly checks, monthly report | 3,000–5,000 / month (ad budget paid by the client directly, minimum 5,000/month) |

**Bundles to offer:**
- **Starter:** listing fix + one-page site = **8,000–11,000**.
- **Grow:** one-page site + content starter for 3 months = about **6,000 once + 6,000 a month**.

## How to quote
1. **Never quote on the first call.** Send the free audit first, then quote in the meeting once you know their goal.
2. **Give 2 options,** for example "Starter 9,000" and "Grow 6,000 + 6,000 a month". People pick between your options instead of deciding whether to buy at all.
3. **Tie the price to one result:** "One extra patient a week pays for this."
4. **Payment:**
   - 50% deposit before you start and 50% on delivery.
   - Monthly plans are paid at the start of each month, with a 3-month minimum.
   - Accept InstaPay or bank transfer, and get a written scope on WhatsApp at least.
5. **Never discount.** Remove scope instead: "For 6,000 I can do the site without the booking page."
6. **Ad budgets** are always paid by the client to Meta or Google, never from your card.
