# Prompt Library

Templates in the playbook's anatomy order. Replace [brackets]. Always attach the real product photos as references for product work. Keep the cast varied.

## Product stills
**Perfume — Gulf souq**
"Close product shot on a Sony A7 IV with a 90mm macro at f/5.6. The [brand] perfume bottle exactly as in the reference image, same label, cap and glass colour, stands on a dark oud-wood shelf in a Deira souq perfume shop. Behind it, out of focus, are mirrored shelves of other bottles and warm fluorescent shop light. A small hard light from the upper left makes a bright edge on the glass and a soft reflection on the wood. There are fingerprints on the glass, a few dust specks, and a price tag slightly out of focus at the edge of the frame. True-to-life colours, no retouching, no text."

**Abaya — editorial in a real place**
"Full-length fashion photo on a 50mm lens at f/2.8. A [age] [Gulf/Egyptian] woman, natural makeup and visible skin texture, wears the [brand] abaya exactly as in the reference: same fabric, embroidery and cut. She walks past a sunlit villa entrance in [Riyadh/Dubai] at 4 pm. Warm side light, long shadow on a limestone wall. The fabric moves naturally, with creases at the elbows and a slight wind at the hem. Candid, mid-step, not looking at the camera. Subtle film grain, muted colours."

**Jewelry — macro on skin**
"Macro shot on a 100mm lens. The [brand] necklace exactly as in the reference rests on the collarbone of a woman in her [30s]. Real skin texture with fine hairs and a small mole. Small hard light creates sparkle points on the stones. Black knit top, blurred warm interior behind. A slight focus falloff at the edges. No retouching."

**Skincare — bathroom shelf**
"Shot on iPhone 1x lens. The [brand] serum bottle exactly as in the reference sits on a real bathroom shelf in a Cairo apartment, next to a toothbrush cup, a hair tie and a half-used tube. Morning light through a frosted window on the right. A few water droplets on the bottle, slight reflection in the mirror edge. Everyday snapshot, slightly tilted framing."

**Food — Egyptian restaurant table**
"Overhead shot on a 35mm lens, iPhone-like colour. A [dish] on a real restaurant table in [area], with a half-drunk glass of tea, crumpled napkins, bread pieces and a phone at the edge of the frame. Steam rising, sauce drips on the plate rim. Warm lamp light from the top right, slight shadow from a hand reaching in. No food styling perfection."

## Video shots (image-to-video, from an approved keyframe)
**Product reveal (3–5 s)**
"Locked-off tripod. A hand enters from the right, picks up the [product] and turns it a quarter so the label faces the camera over 2 seconds. Natural speed, small reflection shift on the glass, the shadow follows the hand. No camera movement, no slow motion."

**UGC talking ad (one shot of a sequence, 4 s)**
"Vertical, iPhone front camera at arm's length, handheld with slight natural shake. A [age] [Egyptian/Gulf] [man/woman] in a lived-in bedroom, window light from the left, speaks to the camera and lifts the [product] into frame on the word '[word]'. Real speaking pace, small head movements, a blink, eyes briefly off the lens. Room tone and slight echo."

**Lifestyle B-roll (4 s)**
"Handheld 35mm. [Person] walks out of a [Maadi café / Dubai mall entrance] holding the [product], quick natural pace, the camera follows slightly behind and to the side, small reframe. Street sounds, a car horn far away. Afternoon sun, lens flare as they pass a window."

**Cinematic concept hook (one idea, 5 s)**
"Sony FX3, 35mm, f/2, 24 fps. [Setup in a real place]. At second 3, [the one impossible moment, described physically]. Everything else stays ordinary and real. Motivated light from [source]. Subtle grain. No glow effects beyond the one moment."

## Character sheet (for recurring people)
"Character reference sheet, same person in three photos side by side: front, three-quarter, profile. [Age], [region], [hair], [distinguishing features: small scar on eyebrow, freckles, gap in teeth], wearing [wardrobe]. Neutral grey backdrop, soft even light, natural skin texture, no retouching."

## Negative / style lock (append)
"No retouching, no plastic skin, no cinematic teal-orange grade, no slow motion, no text, no watermark, no extra fingers, no duplicated people, no warped background lines."
