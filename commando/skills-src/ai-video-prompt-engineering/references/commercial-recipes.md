# Commercial Hero Recipes (Mode B: "scroll-stopping ad")

The look in top AI product ads: **photoreal materials, staged like a big-budget studio shoot.** The product is the hero, bright and crisp, with one strong visual idea. It is not documentary realism. It reads as "a luxury brand paid for this shoot".

## The 7 rules of Mode B
1. **Product = 35–60% of the frame**, centred or on the rule of thirds, label facing camera, sharp edge to edge (f/8–f/11).
2. **Exposure is bright.** High-key or rich colour; even "dark luxury" uses a strong rim light so the product glows. Never muddy or underexposed.
3. **Studio light is named.** Big softbox key (upper left), rim/back light on glass edges, fill on the shadow side, sometimes a hard spot through the liquid.
4. **One visual idea per image:** splash, levitation, ingredient world, podium in nature, colour-block set, macro texture, scale play. Not three at once.
5. **The props come from the product's own story** (notes, ingredients, origin, the brand name's meaning). Mayura = peacock → feathers, teal/emerald/gold. Oud → wood, amber, resin. Citrus → sliced fruit, water splash.
6. **Clean backgrounds:** a gradient, a single colour, or nature in soft focus. No clutter, receipts or fingerprints (those are Mode A).
7. **Text and graphics go in after** (Canva / commando-design-studio). Never ask the model for headlines.

Prompt opener for Mode B: `High-end commercial product photography, [Phase One / Sony A1], [90–100mm macro], f/8–f/11, crisp focus edge to edge.`
Style closer: `Bright, clean, rich colour, glossy magazine advertising look.`
Negative add-on: `no dark or moody lighting, no muddy exposure, no plastic CGI look, no invented text.`

## Recipe list (fill the [brackets]; keep exact counts)

1. **Pedestal + story props:** product on a [stone/marble/sandstone] pedestal, exactly [N] [story prop] arranged behind or around it, soft gradient background in the brand's colours.
2. **Frozen splash:** product floating or standing, one clean ribbon of [liquid matching the product] with exactly [N] droplets, high-speed flash, gradient background.
3. **Ingredient world:** product surrounded by its real notes or ingredients, sliced or whole ([N] pieces each), soft daylight, cream or pastel background.
4. **Nature podium ("the product that grows"):** product on a mossy rock or stone in a sunlit forest/garden, soft god-rays, ferns and exactly [N] small flowers, shallow background blur.
5. **Gulf luxury landscape:** product on a sand dune crest at golden hour, backlit so the liquid glows, long shadow, clear sky.
6. **Colour-block set:** product on a solid-colour block in the brand colour, hard shadow from a single spotlight, a matching coloured background, minimal and graphic.
7. **Water surface:** product half-submerged or standing in still water, ripples, reflection, bright sky or studio gradient.
8. **Macro texture hero:** extreme close-up of the cap, label or glass detail, with sparkle points and a shallow depth of field.
9. **Scale play:** product at giant scale in a real place (a monument-sized bottle in a mall atrium or desert), still photoreal light. Use rarely, for hero concepts only.
10. **Dark luxury done right:** black background, but strong rim light, a warm spot through the liquid and gold reflections. The product must glow, never sink into shadow.

## Video (Mode B)
Same beat formula. Use slow, calm hero moves (push-in, slow orbit with the ring/halo guard, gentle levitation, light sweep across the glass). One reveal per clip: liquid glows, feathers open, splash freezes, light sweeps.

## Choosing the mode
| The client needs… | Mode |
|---|---|
| Instagram grid, ads, launch posts, e-commerce hero, "make it wow" | **B: commercial hero** |
| UGC ads, "real customer" vibe (as an ad), behind the scenes, social trust | **A: real life** (playbook) |
| Spec sample for cold outreach to a shop | **B first** (it impresses); add one A image if they care about authenticity |
