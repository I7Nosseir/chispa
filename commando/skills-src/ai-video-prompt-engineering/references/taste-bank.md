# Taste Bank (the founder's visual references, decoded)

Source: the Canva design "Taste Bank" (one reference per page plus a one-line note), or batches of screenshots in chat.
Each reference is decoded into reusable parts. Before writing any prompt, pick the 1–2 closest entries and copy their light, composition and palette.
