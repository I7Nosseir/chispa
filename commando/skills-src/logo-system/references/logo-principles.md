# Logo principles reference

Contents: 1. Logo types · 2. Paul Rand's criteria (scoring guide) · 3. Typeface families and what they say · 4. Clichés by category · 5. Construction craft · 6. Misuse list · 7. Egypt notes · Sources

## 1. Logo types
| Type | What it is | Strength | Weakness | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wordmark (logotype) | the full name set in type | name recall; elegant for short names | weak at tiny sizes if long | Google, Coca-Cola, Chanel |
| Lettermark | initials only | compact, works small | meaningless until known | HBO, CNN |
| Monogram | letters fused or stylised into one mark | luxury heritage, ownable | can become illegible | LV, YSL, Gucci GG |
| Signet / seal | monogram inside a ring/seal | authority, tradition, "approved" | gets busy if text goes in the ring | university seals, signet rings |
| Emblem | text *inside* a badge | heritage, institutional | fails small; hard to separate | Starbucks (old), Harley-Davidson |
| Combination mark | symbol + wordmark | explains + identifies; easier to trademark | needs a lockup system | Adidas, Lacoste |
| Pictorial / abstract | an image or shape | instant recognition once famous | expensive to teach meaning | Apple, Nike |
| Masthead | newspaper-style name | editorial authority | can look dated | The Economist, FT |
| Signature | handwritten name | personal, warm | fails small, blur, embroidery; often illegible | personal sign-offs |

## 2. Paul Rand's criteria (score 1–5)
- **Distinctive** — 5: could not be mistaken for any competitor; 1: generic template look.
- **Visible** — 5: reads from across a room / at a glance in a feed; 1: needs zooming.
- **Adaptable** — 5: works one-colour, reversed, embroidered, foil, favicon; 1: only works in full colour large.
- **Memorable** — 5: someone can sketch it after one look; 1: no clear shape to remember.
- **Universal** — 5: no problematic readings in target cultures/languages; 1: risky meaning.
- **Timeless** — 5: no trend effects; would look right in 10 years; 1: trend-of-the-year.
- **Simple** — 5: one idea, few parts; 1: many competing elements.
Plus: **Fit** (personality, audience) and **Story** (a one-sentence meaning the owner can retell).

| Family | Feeling | Good OFL options (Google Fonts) |
|---|---|---|
| Didone / high-contrast serif | fashion, luxury, editorial, glamour | Bodoni Moda, Playfair Display, Prata |
| Roman capitals (inscriptional) | authority, permanence, classical, institutions | Cinzel, Cormorant SC, Marcellus |

## 4. Clichés by category (avoid unless subverted on purpose)
- **Education/tutoring:** graduation cap, open book, pencil, lightbulb, owl, apple, globe, rocket.
- **Business/economics:** rising arrow, bar chart, dollar sign, handshake, briefcase.
- **Luxury:** generic crown, laurel wreath, diamond icon, gold gradient text.
- **Personal brands:** initials in a plain circle with a thin sans; face silhouettes.

## 5. Construction craft
- Optical corrections: round letters (O, C, S) overshoot baseline/cap height ~1–2%; centre visually, not by bounding box.
- All-caps wordmarks: open the tracking (0.08–0.2 em) and kern pairs manually (AV, RA, KH).
- Stroke floor: thinnest stroke ≥ 2% of mark height (else it vanishes at 32 px).
- Responsive logos: define simplified versions for small sizes (drop inner rings, taglines, descriptors).
- Clear space: use a letter or part of the mark as the unit (e.g., the "R" height).
- Build masters in a single colour first; colour is applied by the system, not baked into the idea.

## 6. Misuse list (include in every delivery)
Don't stretch or squash · don't rotate · don't recolour outside the approved pairs · don't add outlines, shadows, glows, bevels · don't place on busy photos without a solid plate · don't rearrange lockup parts · don't use the tagline lockup below its minimum size · don't animate the mark's letters apart · don't put the logo at the end of videos (founder's rule — a signature moment or line ends videos instead).

## 7. Egypt notes
- Trademarks: Egyptian Trademarks & Industrial Designs Office under ITDA; online search covers **word elements only** (itda.gov.eg) — figurative marks can't be searched, so also check visually against competitors; registration takes ~18 months; use a local lawyer.
- Arabic lockups: if an Arabic version is needed, design it as a partner (matched weight and height), not a translation squeezed underneath.
- Avoid shapes or letter combinations that read as religious symbols or political emblems; check Arabic letterforms for unintended words.
